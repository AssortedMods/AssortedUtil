# Assorted Graves

Where you die, everything you carried and all your experience go into a grave. Use it to get
everything back into the slots it came from, armour included. Breaking it spills it all instead.
Explosions, pistons and bosses cannot move or break a grave.

Items with Curse of Vanishing still vanish, and a totem of undying keeps you out of a grave.
From the Grim Util part of the old [Grim Pack](https://github.com/grim3212/grim-pack).

Settings - experience, who can open a grave and how far to look for room - are in `assortedgraves-common.toml`.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib). One of the mods built from
[AssortedUtil](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedUtil); see its README for building and
reporting issues.
