# Assorted Double Doors

One click opens both doors of a pair, a whole trapdoor hatch, or fence gates stacked on or beside
each other. An iron partner still wants redstone. Modded doors, trapdoors and gates work too, and
the `assorteddoubledoors:ignored_by_double_doors` block tag leaves any of them out. Sneak to open
just one. From the Grim Util part of the old [Grim Pack](https://github.com/grim3212/grim-pack).

Settings - doors, trapdoors and fence gates each on or off, and how many open at once - are in `assorteddoubledoors-common.toml`.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib). One of the mods built from
[AssortedUtil](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedUtil); see its README for building and
reporting issues.
