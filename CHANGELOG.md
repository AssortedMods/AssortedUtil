# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.2 on NeoForge and Fabric. Graves, double doors, the item replacer
and time come from Grim Pack's Grim Util (1.12).

- Graves put each item back in the slot it came from, armour included, and keep every point of
  experience.
- Items with Curse of Vanishing still vanish, and a totem of undying keeps you out of a grave.
- Double doors to open trapdoors, doors, and fence gates all together.
- Automatic Item Replacer for when tools break or you run out of a block  or item and have a replacement
- Damage numbers show the damage actually taken, after armour, and name the damage that caused it.
- Time keeps its 1.12 panel, now at the top left, as status effect icons fill the top right.
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0.
